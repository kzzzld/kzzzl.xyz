Omarchy is a AI-centric Linux distro made by DHH using Arch Linux. It uses software like Hyprland, Quickshell, Foot etc. It's popular because it helps people use Hyprland easily without configuring it.

## What I think about Omarchy?

I think Omarchy Linux is good, as a dotfile. I think Omarchy does not deserve being a distro but it's a good starting point for dotfiles.

## Should we give AI access to our computers?

I think yes but if it's running locally. Giving full access to your system to a cloud AI means giving full access to your system to a cloud. If that cloud targets you, they can do everything with your computer.

We should not give that much privilege to AI agents.
