I know. Lust can be so strong. Sometimes, it feels stronger than anything else in the world. But, you are a human and you must have strong will. Your lust will try to tempt you but you will try harder and use willpower against your lust.

## Your Lust is not your friend

Lust can destroy your life. We saw that from examples like; [Jensen Huang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jensen_Huang#Scandal_and_resignation_(2026)), [Deniz Baykal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deniz_Baykal?utm_source=chatgpt.com#Leader_of_the_Opposition,_2002%E2%80%932010) etc.

## Lust can destroy your faith

I'm a Muslim. But if you are Jew, Christian or Muslim, we all believe in god. Lust makes you uncomfortable in your prayers because you always think about that stuff.

## Your lust will mostly be there

Your lust will mostly be there. It will be hard but you should fight with your urges. Eventually, you will win and happy when you die inshallah.

## Conclusion

Don't let some temporary pleasure destroy your whole life both before and after death.
