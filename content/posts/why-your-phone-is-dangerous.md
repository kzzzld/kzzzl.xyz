## Little Microphones and Cameras Everywhere

Did you read [1984](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nineteen_Eighty-Four)? It's a dystopian novel. In that novel, there's camera and microphone in every room. They can even hear your heart rate. Now, we have phones which has microphone and camera. We have smartwatches counting our heart rate. That dystopia is just being real.

## You are the Product

Just think, it something is free, then how they make money on it? Most of monopolistic apps are selling your data to companies that show ad to you.

## Metadata

A photo or message can be encrypted. But where did you send it, how did you send it, which file type etc. are just unencrypted in most of the messaging apps.

## Capitalism in your Pocket

You may be a capitalist or not but we agree on something, your phone imposes capitalism. Someone who has money can watch YouTube without ads but poor guy can't do it. Or a rich guy can play expensive games but poor guy can't.

You're letting an ideology be in your most private parts in your life.

## The Illusion of Choice

Wow, you use Android because you want freedom? Sadly, Android is going to restrict APK installations. So there's no freedom but you feel like you are free.

## The Right to Repair

I think every device should be repairable. That's why I'm a fan of old ThinkPads. But your phone breaks warranty even if you change a battery without giving some money to that company.
